ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp. vegetable oil or lard
  • 2 Thick bacon slices diced
  • 1 Chorizo crumbled (about 2 oz.)
  • ½ Lb. pork meat cut into small cubes
  • 1 Lb. sirloin or ribeye cut into small cubes*
  • 1 cup diced white onion
  • 1 green bell pepper diced
  • 2 serrano peppers diced
  • 2 hot dog sausages chopped
  • 4 cups of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 cup beer room temperature
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • ⅓ cup chopped cilantro

instructions

  1. In a large frying pan, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Once hot, add the bacon and cook until crispy. Remove and place on a plate to drain excess fat.

  2. Next, in the same frying pan, cook the chorizo, about 5-6 minutes. Remove and place on the plate with the bacon.

  3. Now, add the pork meat and cook for about 7 minutes and then remove. Don't worry if it doesn't yet look well done, it will finish cooking with the rest of the ingredients.

  4. Add the beef to the frying pan and cook for 8 minutes. Remove and place with the other already cooked ingredients.

  5. Stir in the diced onion and peppers in the frying pan and cook for about 3 minutes.

  6. Add the already cooked meat and the diced tomatoes. Cook for about 10 minutes (by this time, the tomatoes will start releasing their juices). Next, pour the beer, season with salt and pepper, and cover. Keep cooking for another 5 minutes until all the meats are soft and well cooked.

  7. Just before serving, stir in the chopped cilantro.
